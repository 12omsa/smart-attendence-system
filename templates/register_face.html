📷 AI Attendance System

Face Registration

Register a student's face for AI-based attendance recognition.

Registration Instructions

  1. Enter the student's ID.
  2. Enter the student's full name.
  3. Click Start Face Registration.
  4. The webcam will open automatically.
  5. Keep exactly one face visible.
  6. Press SPACE to capture a face sample.
  7. Capture 10 samples.
  8. Slightly change your head position between samples.
  9. Press Q if you want to cancel.
Important: Make sure no other application is using the webcam while registering the face.
